SPIDER-MAN

Spider-Man has swung his way from the comics into various forms of media over the years, like video games and blockbuster movies!

Iconic moments from over 60+ years of web-slinging history!
  • Spider-Man's First Appearance (Amazing Fantasy #15, 1962) The debut of Spider-Man (Peter Parker) in Amazing Fantasy #15, created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, marked the beginning of one of the most iconic superhero characters in comic history.
  • The Death of Gwen Stacy (The Amazing Spider-Man #121-122, 1973) One of the most significant and tragic moments in Spider-Man's history is the death of his girlfriend, Gwen Stacy, at the hands of the Green Goblin, Norman Osborn. This event changed Peter's life forever.
  • The "Black Suit" / Symbiote Saga (The Amazing Spider-Man #252, 1984) Peter Parker acquires a new black suit (which is later revealed to be the symbiote Venom), marking the beginning of a new phase for Spider-Man and the introduction of the Venom character.
  • The Marriage of Peter Parker and Mary Jane Watson (The Amazing Spider-Man #290, 1987) After years of romance and challenges, Peter Parker marries Mary Jane Watson in a landmark moment for Spider-Man's personal life.
  • The Rise of Venom (The Amazing Spider-Man #300, 1988) The first appearance of Eddie Brock as Venom, one of Spider-Man's most dangerous and iconic enemies, marked a major shift in the Spider-Man rogues' gallery.
  • The Clone Saga (1994-1996) A massive storyline that introduced Ben Reilly, a clone of Peter Parker. This arc led to the temporary replacement of Peter Parker by Ben as Spider-Man and had lasting effects on Spider-Man’s continuity.
  • "The Other: Evolve or Die" (2005-2006) This crossover event saw Peter Parker facing death and being resurrected with new powers. It marked the beginning of a more mystical aspect in Spider-Man's mythos.
  • The "One More Day" Storyline (2007) In this controversial arc, Peter Parker and Mary Jane make a pact with Mephisto to erase their marriage in exchange for saving Aunt May's life. This event led to a major reset in Peter's life and altered Spider-Man's status quo for years.
  • The Death of Peter Parker (Ultimate Fallout #6, 2011) In the Ultimate Universe (Earth-1610), Peter Parker dies heroically, which leads to Miles Morales becoming the new Spider-Man. This event was a turning point in the Ultimate Spider-Man continuity.
  • Spider-Verse (2014-2015) A multiverse-spanning event that brought together numerous versions of Spider-Man from across different universes, including Miles Morales, Spider-Gwen, and Spider-Man 2099, to battle Morlun and the Inheritors.
